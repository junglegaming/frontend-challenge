### Skills to Know About
- /speckit-specify, /speckit-clarify — write and tighten a feature spec.
- /speckit-plan — turn a spec into a plan (research, data model, contracts, quickstart).
- /speckit-tasks — generate the ordered task list from the plan.
- /speckit-analyze — check spec, plan and tasks agree before coding.
- /speckit-implement — execute tasks from `tasks.md` and tick them off.
- /speckit-constitution — amend `.specify/memory/constitution.md` (governance rules, e.g. what `apps/api` may use).
- /plan — plan mode for work outside the Spec Kit flow.
